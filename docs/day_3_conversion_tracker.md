## 1. Allowed Status Taxonomy
- **`NEW`**: Lead identified in target segment, not yet contacted.
- **`CONTACTED`**: Initial message or catalog preview transmitted.
- **`REPLIED`**: Customer acknowledged or asked an initial question.
- **`QUALIFIED`**: Occasion, fabric preference, and budget confirmed.
- **`PRODUCT_SHARED`**: Tailored recommendation card and daylight photos sent.
- **`FOLLOW_UP`**: Scheduled for subsequent consultation / awaiting daylight review.
- **`CHECKOUT_STARTED`**: Payment link / bank details / invoice shared for ordering.
- **`ORDERED`**: Order confirmed and invoice issued.
- **`NOT_INTERESTED`**: Politely declined or looking for unstocked categories.
- **`DO_NOT_CONTACT`**: Customer requested suppression (opt-out).
