## 1. Status Taxonomy & Quality Gates
- **`CHECKOUT_STARTED`**: Payment instructions / invoice generated; awaiting customer payment transfer and verified UTR receipt.
- **`ORDERED`**: Used **ONLY** when verified payment evidence and order creation record exist. *(Customer intent, requesting payment details, or verbal confirmation do NOT qualify).*
- **`PRODUCT_SHARED`**: Product recommendation cards, daylight media, or spec sheets delivered.
- **`QUALIFIED`**: Occasion, fabric preference, and budget confirmed.
- **`FOLLOW_UP`**: Scheduled for subsequent consultation.
- **`REPLIED`**: Customer acknowledged or asked an initial question.
- **`NOT_INTERESTED`**: Politely declined or looking for unstocked categories.
- **`DO_NOT_CONTACT`**: Customer requested suppression.
